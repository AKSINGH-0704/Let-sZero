## Selection rationale

The reducer retained only records with a standalone troubleshooting, implementation, governance, decision, reference, or test job; concrete HTTP evidence; and a bounded audience/problem. It removed parent-child, cross-provider, cross-problem, and canonical-duplicate ideas through the cluster review decisions and prior Wave 1/corpus comparisons. Unknown provisional navigation labels were set to null rather than inventing collections or paths.

## Evidence limits

Measured demand metrics are unavailable. The supplied materials do not provide traffic, rankings, search volume, conversion data, or validated business-impact measurements. Evidence classes therefore describe source character, not demand. Provider and regulatory guidance may change; revalidate before publication.
