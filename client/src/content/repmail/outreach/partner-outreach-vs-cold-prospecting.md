# Partner Outreach vs. Cold Prospecting: Key Differences

Partner outreach and cold prospecting can use the same inbox, but they are different jobs. **Partner outreach** starts with a collaboration hypothesis—referral, integration, event, or co-marketing. **Cold prospecting** starts with a product or service hypothesis for a recipient with no established relationship. Classify the message by its purpose and recipient context before choosing a template or automation.

## Use a decision tree

Ask three questions. First, has the recipient or organization had a relevant relationship with you? Record the source and date rather than relying on memory. Second, is the proposed message primarily about working together, or is it an offer to buy your service? Third, does the message include a referral fee, affiliate arrangement, free access, or another material connection? If any answer is unclear, route it to a human reviewer.

The classification is operational, not an exemption. The FTC says commercial email requirements apply to business-to-business messages, while the ICO says UK rules differ for individuals, sole traders, and corporate bodies [1] [2]. A partner label does not remove the need for truthful identity, a valid contact path, or an appropriate opt-out.

## Change the workflow, not just the copy

For partner outreach, preserve the research note: the shared audience, the concrete collaboration, the proposed owner, and the next step. If a payment or other value is involved, make the relationship clear where the endorsement or recommendation could affect how it is evaluated [3]. Do not describe an integration, event, or referral as agreed until the other party confirms it.

For cold prospecting, record the business signal, role, source, message purpose, and suppression status. Keep the ask narrow and make it easy to decline. Link the campaign to a [research-first partnership template](/repmail/learn/outreach/partnership-outreach-email-template) only when the recipient actually fits a partnership hypothesis. For a product pitch, use the [cold-email unsubscribe checklist](/repmail/learn/compliance/cold-email-unsubscribe-requirements).

Measure the lanes separately. Partner replies may reflect relationship context, while cold replies reflect a different denominator and message promise. Do not merge them into one benchmark. RepMail can help keep sending events and suppression visible, but it cannot determine the relationship or purpose for you.
