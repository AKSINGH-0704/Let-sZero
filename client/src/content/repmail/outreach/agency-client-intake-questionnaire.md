A useful agency intake questionnaire produces decisions the delivery team can act on: who to contact, why the message is relevant, who must be excluded, what may be claimed, and who can approve changes. Copy the worksheet below into the client workspace and mark each answer **confirmed**, **needs evidence**, or **blocked**.

## Client intake worksheet

| Status | Question | Required evidence or answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ☐ | What business conversation should the campaign start? | One outcome and a definition of a qualified reply |
| ☐ | Which companies and roles fit the ICP? | Firmographic and role boundaries, with exclusions |
| ☐ | Which regions, languages, or recipient types are out of scope? | Country, sector, customer, competitor, and personal-address exclusions |
| ☐ | What problem and offer are being presented? | Plain-language offer and a contact-appropriate CTA |
| ☐ | Which claims and proof points are approved? | Source, owner, date, and permitted wording |
| ☐ | Where did the audience data come from? | Provider/source, collection date, validation and use constraints |
| ☐ | Which contacts must never be mailed? | Existing customers, opt-outs, complaints, competitors, and internal exclusions |
| ☐ | Who approves audience, copy, identity, and launch? | Named primary and backup for each decision |
| ☐ | What sender identity will recipients recognize? | From name, domain, reply path, postal address, and escalation mailbox |
| ☐ | What counts as a stop signal? | Complaint, bounce pattern, provider notice, factual error, or client request |

## Field triage

**Required fields** block launch when blank: objective, ICP, exclusions, data provenance, sender identity, opt-out owner, and approvers. **Optional fields** can improve context but should not delay a safe first test. **Red flags** route to review: personal addresses in a B2B list, unverifiable claims, unclear ownership of the list, no suppression source, or a request to hide the sender.

The [personalization data checklist](/repmail/learn/cold-email/personalization-data-checklist) adds source, freshness, relevance, and fallback checks after the questionnaire. The [list verification guide](/repmail/learn/cold-email/build-and-verify-a-cold-email-list) covers the separate question of whether an address is deliverable.

## Decision-rights record

| Decision | Client approves | Agency prepares | Escalate when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Audience and exclusions | ☐ | ☐ | ICP or suppression conflict |
| Claims and proof | ☐ | ☐ | Source is missing or stale |
| Sequence and CTA | ☐ | ☐ | Message differs from approved offer |
| Launch | ☐ | ☐ | Any required field remains blocked |

Do not infer that an answered questionnaire is a compliance approval. Record uncertainty beside the field and route jurisdictional or contractual questions to the appropriate reviewer.
