# Cold Email Tool Cost Calculator: What to Include

A cold email tool cost calculator should model total cost of ownership, not just the subscription line. Enter your own quantities for seats, mailboxes, domains, verification, prospect data, infrastructure, integrations, and labor. The result is a planning model, not a prediction of savings or campaign performance.

Treat every commercial input as configuration-dependent: pricing, the definition of a billable seat, currency, billing term, renewal date or mode, and autopay behavior can depend on the workspace and configuration unless the current server-authoritative commercial state says otherwise. Use a current dated quote or checkout state for those fields, and do not turn a modelled renewal date into an automatic renewal or next-charge promise. RepMail's current seat catalog is an INR/India catalog, not a global USD price list; do not generalize or convert it into one.

## A practical way to evaluate total cost of ownership

1. **Choose a time period and currency, then document whether each value is monthly, annual, or one-time; record the quoted billing term and any explicitly stated renewal or autopay behavior separately.**
2. **Enter quantities and unit costs for the tool, seats, mailboxes, domains, verification, data, and integrations.**
3. **Add implementation, review, reply handling, and compliance operations as hours multiplied by an internal rate.**
4. **Run base, growth, and idle-month scenarios; label every vendor quote and assumption with its date.**

## Decision table

| Input | Formula | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Software | plan + add-ons | Separates included limits from extras |
| Sending setup | mailboxes + domains | Shows identity and lifecycle cost |
| Data | records + verification | Captures list creation and refresh |
| Operations | hours × internal rate | Makes human work visible |

## Edge cases and limits

Do not populate missing vendor prices with a benchmark. If a plan is quote-only, leave the cell blank or use a documented range. A calculator also cannot prove inbox placement, reply rate, or compliance suitability.

## Where RepMail fits

Use the worksheet to compare RepMail or any other sending layer on the same assumptions; the useful output is an auditable scenario, not a winner chosen by sticker price.
